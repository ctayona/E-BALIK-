### Behaviour decisions (where the prompt left room)
- Validity (`valid_until`) starts when staff approve, not when the user submits.
- Rejecting a **first registration** resets the tag to blank (owner, name, photo cleared) so the sticker can be registered again; the owner is notified with the reason.
- Rejecting a **new photo** deletes it and restores the previous photo and previous status (`active` or `lost`); the approved photo stays in `item_image_url` until the new one is approved, so rejection never leaves a tag without a photo.
- A pending tag cannot be marked lost, renewed or reported found; finders see only that the tag exists.
- Admin edits go through a separate `PATCH` endpoint that never touches the approval fields.
