### Edit Audit
**Priority 1: one-time codes**
- `Server/app/utils/otp_guard.py` (new): the rules. 60 second cooldown per email and 5 codes per email per 24 hours, counted from `otp_tokens` (falls back to an in-memory limiter when the table cannot be read); 12 code requests per 10 minutes per network address; disposable email domains (about 100, subdomains count); a per-address limit on verification attempts; `register_failure` retires a code on the fifth wrong guess (counted in `otp_tokens.attempts`, or in memory until the migration is run).
